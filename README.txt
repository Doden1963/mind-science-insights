Additional philosophical reflection:

One interesting question in early Buddhism is whether Nirvana should necessarily be understood as absolutely eternal and irreversible.

The historical Buddha appears to have taught Nirvana as a definitive liberation from samsara.
However, one may still philosophically ask how a human being could know with complete certainty that all causes of rebirth had permanently ceased.

Buddhist scholar Richard Francis Gombrich generally argues that the historical Buddha probably himself believed Nirvana to be definitive,
but modern people may still legitimately discuss whether later traditions made some descriptions more metaphysically absolute over time.

Perhaps Nirvana truly is definitive.
Perhaps it was experienced as an extremely deep transformation of consciousness that felt absolute.
It may simply be difficult to know with certainty.
